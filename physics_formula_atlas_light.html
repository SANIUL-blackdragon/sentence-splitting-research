Intensity, amplitude & inverse square

Power spread through area
Definition

Intensity

I=PA

Units: I = W m⁻² · P = W · A = m².

Square-law

Intensity and amplitude

I∝A2

Doubling amplitude raises intensity by a factor of 4.

Inverse square

Point-source intensity

I=P4πr2

Radiation spreads over a spherical area, so intensity falls as distance squared.

I ∝ 1/r²

Effective intensity on an angled surface

04

Refraction, Snell’s law & TIR

Index ↔ speed ↔ angle
Definition

Refractive index

n=cv

n: dimensionless · c: vacuum light speed · v: material light speed.

n ∝ 1/v
Higher n means slower light in the material.
Snell

Refraction angle relation

n1sinθ1=n2sinθ2

Angles are measured from the normal. Entering a denser medium means n₂ > n₁ and the ray bends towards the normal.

Critical angle

Critical-angle condition

sinC=n2n1

For light into air/vacuum: sin C = 1/n.

TIR needs n₁ > n₂ and incidence angle > C.
